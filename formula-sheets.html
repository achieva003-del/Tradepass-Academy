Trade Formula Sheets

The math your trade actually tests: layout, area, volume, stairs, roofs and conversions on one clean reference. Use the print button for a copy that fits in your toolbox.

Right angles & layout

Pythagorean theoremc = √(a² + b²), the length of the diagonal (hypotenuse)
3-4-5 square checkMeasure 3 units along one side, 4 along the other; the diagonal is exactly 5 if the corner is square
Diagonal of a squared = side × 1.414

Area & volume

RectangleA = L × W
TriangleA = ½ × b × h
CircleA = π × r²
Concrete volumeV = L × W × D. Keep all three in metres for m³

Stairs

Unit riseunit rise = total rise ÷ number of risers
TreadsNumber of treads = number of risers − 1 (typical straight run)
Code limitsMaximum rise and minimum run are set by the building code in force. Always confirm the current values for your jurisdiction

Roofs

SlopeExpressed as unit rise per 12 units of run (e.g. 6/12 rises 6 in per 12 in of run)
Rafter line lengthline length = √(run² + rise²)

Board measure

Board feetBF = (T″ × W″ × L′) ÷ 12, with thickness and width in inches, length in feet

Metric ↔ imperial conversions

Length1 in = 25.4 mm  ·  1 ft = 304.8 mm  ·  1 m = 3.281 ft
Area1 m² = 10.764 ft²
Temperature°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
