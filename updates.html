Academy Updates

Exam changes, code notices and new study material, published as they happen so nothing on exam day surprises you.

Exam ChangesJuly 2026

2026 Red Seal Blueprint Changes

Red Seal exam blueprints are revised on a rolling basis as occupational standards are updated, and the weighting of questions across major work activities can shift between cycles. If your trade's standard was revised for 2026, the proportion of questions you'll see from each block may differ from older prep material.

Our guidance: pull the current exam breakdown for your trade before you build a study schedule, and let those weightings decide where your hours go. TradePass study guides and the free blueprint checklists on this site follow the current published standard for each trade.

Always confirm the current Occupational Standard and exam breakdown for your trade at red-seal.ca — it is the official source.

Code UpdatesJune 2026

CEC Code Updates for Electrical Candidates

The Canadian Electrical Code is amended on a regular cycle, and provinces adopt new editions on their own timelines. For 309A and 442A candidates this matters twice: the exam tests code application, and the edition in force on your jobsite may differ from the one you studied.

Our upcoming Construction Electrician 309A and Industrial Electrician 442A guides are being written against the current CEC edition, and their companion resources will be revised as amendments land — that's what "lifetime resource updates" means in practice.

Check which CEC edition your province has adopted with your provincial electrical safety authority before your exam sitting.

New MaterialMay 2026

New Practice Questions in the 403A Companion Bank

The free companion resources for the Red Seal Carpenter 403A guide continue to grow. The latest additions focus on the areas readers ask about most: stair calculations, formwork, and questions that apply current building code requirements.

If you own the guide, the QR codes inside your book always point to the latest version of every resource — nothing to re-download, nothing extra to buy. Start with the free sample practice test to see the format.

RoadmapApril 2026

Upcoming Study Guides

Four more Red Seal trades are in production: Construction Electrician 309A and Industrial Electrician 442A are furthest along, with Heavy Equipment Technician 421A and Automotive Service Technician 310S in development with our trade advisors.

Each guide follows the same system as the carpentry title: built from the official Occupational Standard, 500+ practice questions, 10 full-length tests, and a plain-language explanation behind every answer. Join a launch list on the Study Guides section and we'll email you the day your trade ships.
