Red Seal Practice Test — Free Sample

Five multiple-choice questions in real Red Seal exam formatting, with instant scoring and the explanation behind every answer. Drawn from the carpentry question bank — more trades are on the way.

Q1.In standard Canadian residential wall framing, studs are typically spaced at:

Why: 16 in (406 mm) on centre is the standard stud spacing for conventional residential wall framing. Wider spacings are used only where the design specifically allows it.

Q2.What is the minimum end bearing required for a stud on a sole plate?

Why: The minimum end bearing for a stud on a sole plate is 19 mm (3/4 in), which provides adequate support and prevents movement.

Q3.What is the primary function of a header in a wall opening?

Why: A header spans the opening and transfers the load from the wall above to the jack (trimmer) studs on each side of the opening.

Q4.A roof has a slope of 6/12. Over a horizontal run of 24 in, how much does it rise?

Why: A 6/12 slope rises 6 in for every 12 in of run. Over 24 in of run, the rise is 6 × 2 = 12 in.

Q5.How many board feet are in a piece of lumber 2 in thick, 6 in wide and 12 ft long?

Why: Board feet = (thickness in inches × width in inches × length in feet) ÷ 12 = (2 × 6 × 12) ÷ 12 = 12 board feet.
