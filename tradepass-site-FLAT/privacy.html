Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

TradePass Academy ("we", "us") publishes Red Seal exam preparation materials and operates this website. This policy explains what personal information we collect through the site and how we handle it, in keeping with Canadian privacy law, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • Email address — only when you submit it through a newsletter or launch-notification form on this site.
  • Basic technical data — our hosting provider may log standard information such as IP address and browser type to operate and secure the site.

We do not collect payment information. Book purchases are completed on Amazon, under Amazon's own privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To send the updates you asked for: new guide launches, blueprint and code notices, and free study resources.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Who we share it with

Email addresses are stored and processed by the email service provider we use to send newsletters. Service providers may only use your information to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Unsubscribing and your rights

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You may also contact us at any time to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.

Children

This website is intended for apprentices and trades professionals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at tradepassacademy.ca@gmail.com.
